Connect Your Private Google Sheet

How Google Sheets Acts as Your Secure Backend

Your Google Sheet remains 100% PRIVATE to you. The Google Apps Script Web App acts as a secure API bridge that lets this web app read Staff_data, read Locations, and append rows into Attendance_Logs without exposing your Google account or Drive!

Quick 60-Second Setup:
  1. Open your Google Sheet: Click here to open Sheet
  2. Click Extensions > Apps Script in the top menu.
  3. Copy the script from the repo file google_apps_script.js and paste it there.
  4. Click Deploy > New deployment > Select Web app.
  5. Set 'Execute as': Me, and 'Who has access': Anyone.
  6. Click Deploy, copy the generated Web App URL, and paste it above!
